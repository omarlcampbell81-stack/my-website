A free community programme in South East London

A baby shower, reimagined as real preparation.

Birth Loop brings families together around a bespoke board game and companion app. Each card is a decision, a question or a scenario you might meet in pregnancy, labour or the first days at home, so you can talk it through, make choices and hear other people's, in a room that feels more like a gathering than a class.

A registered midwife and a BACP-accredited counsellor are in the room throughout, so clinical questions get answered and feelings get room.

Meet the team

Three things in every session

A game that gets people talking

Scenario cards, choices and conversation make hard topics easier to start, from pain relief and induction to C-sections and feeding.

A registered midwife in the room

Ask the questions you have been saving for an appointment and hear clear, clinical answers.

A BACP-accredited counsellor

Birth can stir up a lot. Support for how you feel, alongside what you need to know.
