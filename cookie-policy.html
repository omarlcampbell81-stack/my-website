Cookies

What this website stores on your device, and how to change your choice.

Our approach

We only set what the site needs to work unless you tell us we can do more. When you first visit, a banner asks you to accept all cookies or keep to essential only. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

What we use

Essential. We remember your cookie choice (a small item called bl_consent, kept for up to 12 months) so we do not ask you on every visit. This does not identify you.

Instagram feed (optional). If you accept, the Instagram feed on our home page is loaded from our feed provider, Behold, which may set its own cookies. If you choose essential only, the feed is not loaded.

Analytics (optional). Where we switch on website analytics, it only runs after you accept, and is used to understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.

Fonts and forms. The site's fonts are served by Google Fonts. Booking and contact forms are processed by Web3Forms, which passes your message to us by email.

Your choice

You can also clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.
