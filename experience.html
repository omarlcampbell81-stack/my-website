An afternoon that changes how birth feels.

What happens in a Birth Loop session, who is in the room and who it is for.

The game

Birth Loop is a bespoke board game with a companion app, designed to turn the things we usually avoid saying out loud into something you can play with. Scenario and question cards cover the full journey, from pregnancy and labour to life at home with a newborn.

Topics include pain relief, induction, C-section and VBAC, water birth, home birth, NICU and premature birth, gestational diabetes, hypertension in pregnancy, feeding, newborn care and postnatal recovery. You choose what you most want to focus on when you book.

The people in the room

A registered midwife is there to answer your clinical questions plainly and honestly.

A BACP-accredited counsellor holds space for the emotional side of preparing for birth, including worries you may not have said out loud before.

The Birth Loop team runs the game, keeps the pace relaxed and makes sure everyone gets a turn.

Meet the team

Who it is for

Women, at any stage of pregnancy

Come on your own to a women-only session, or choose the couples format and bring your birth partner.

Dads and birth partners

Dedicated sessions for the people who will be beside you in labour, focused on how to support, speak up and stay steady.

Anyone supporting someone

Not pregnant yourself? You are welcome. Tell us how you are supporting someone when you book.

The escape room

The Appointment

After the game and a break for food, teams move into a 45-minute escape room built around a story. You follow one woman through her antenatal care, the moment plans change, time in the neonatal unit and recovery at home, and every puzzle is a real decision or a real question from that journey.

Teams crack number ciphers, solve a physical sliding puzzle and open lockboxes by completing activities together. Nobody is scored or ranked, and nobody is asked to speak out loud. It is built to be collaborative, a little bit thrilling and safe for everyone in the room.

How the afternoon runs

Four hours, from 12:00pm to 4:00pm.

1

The game

About 90 minutes with the Birth Loop board game, a midwife and a counsellor at the table.

2

Food, included

An hour to eat, talk and meet the other people in the room. Food is provided, and a crèche is included for the whole afternoon.

3

The escape room

45 minutes of puzzles and padlocks in The Appointment, played in teams.

4

Closing circle

A gentle 30-minute debrief to share what stayed with you. Everyone leaves with a voucher.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Birth Loop sessions are free to attend and everyone is welcome. Food and a crèche are included too.

What is included?

Everything: the game, the escape room, food, a crèche and a voucher are all included at no cost.

Do I need to be pregnant to come?

No. You can attend at any stage of pregnancy, and the booking form has an option for people who are supporting someone else.

What is the escape room?

After the game and a break for food, teams take on a 45-minute escape room called The Appointment. You work together through puzzles, ciphers and padlocks while following one woman's story through pregnancy, complications, the neonatal unit and recovery at home. It is collaborative, not competitive, and nobody is ever asked to speak out loud.

Who will be in the room?

A registered midwife and a BACP-accredited counsellor are in the room throughout, alongside the team who run the game.

Can I bring my birth partner?

At our women-only sessions you can choose the couples format when you book and add your birth partner's details. We also run sessions for dads and birth partners.

Is childcare available?

Yes, a crèche is included at our sessions. Tell us on the booking form if you will need it and how old your children are, so we can make sure there is the right space for them.

What if I have dietary or access needs?

Add them to the booking form. There is a field for dietary requirements and one for additional needs or accessibility requirements.

What happens after I book?

We email you to confirm your place and the venue. If a session is full, we add you to the waitlist.
