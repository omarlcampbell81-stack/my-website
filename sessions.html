Before you book

Is it really free?

Yes. Birth Loop sessions are free to attend and everyone is welcome. Food and a crèche are included too.

What is included?

Everything: the game, the escape room, food, a crèche and a voucher are all included at no cost.

Do I need to be pregnant to come?

No. You can attend at any stage of pregnancy, and the booking form has an option for people who are supporting someone else.

What is the escape room?

After the game and a break for food, teams take on a 45-minute escape room called The Appointment. You work together through puzzles, ciphers and padlocks while following one woman's story through pregnancy, complications, the neonatal unit and recovery at home. It is collaborative, not competitive, and nobody is ever asked to speak out loud.

Who will be in the room?

A registered midwife and a BACP-accredited counsellor are in the room throughout, alongside the team who run the game.

Can I bring my birth partner?

At our women-only sessions you can choose the couples format when you book and add your birth partner's details. We also run sessions for dads and birth partners.

Is childcare available?

Yes, a crèche is included at our sessions. Tell us on the booking form if you will need it and how old your children are, so we can make sure there is the right space for them.

What if I have dietary or access needs?

Add them to the booking form. There is a field for dietary requirements and one for additional needs or accessibility requirements.

What happens after I book?

We email you to confirm your place and the venue. If a session is full, we add you to the waitlist.
