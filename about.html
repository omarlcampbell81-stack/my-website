Built by and for the community it serves.

Birth Loop is a free community health programme created in South East London.

Why Birth Loop exists

Birth Loop began as a game-based community health intervention: a way to help people prepare for birth together, in their own neighbourhood, without it costing them anything.

It was founded by Omar Campbell, who leads the Maternity and Neonatal Voices Partnership at King's College Hospital and sits on the Southwark Maternity Commission. Sessions are shaped by what families tell us they want to know and by the voices that are too often missing from maternity services.

Birth Loop is run by Learn Loop Ltd, a not-for-profit company limited by guarantee.
